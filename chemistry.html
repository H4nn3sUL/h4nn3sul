SCOPE / LIMITS

Built for learning, not plant operation.

These first calculators support a simplified reaction with two reactants and one product. They do not balance arbitrary chemical equations, solve equilibrium or kinetics, design continuous/discontinuous equipment, or include validated thermodynamic/safety models. Never use the outputs for laboratory or industrial operating decisions without a qualified review.
